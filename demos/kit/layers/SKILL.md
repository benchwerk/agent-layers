name: monthly-report
description: Write the monthly expense report. Use when the user asks for a monthly report or a summary of a month's spending.
---
Monthly report format:
1. Title: "Expenses, <Month YYYY>".
2. A table: category, total in euros, share of the month in percent; largest first.
3. One line: the month's total in euros.
Compute from data.json with node; never guess a number.
